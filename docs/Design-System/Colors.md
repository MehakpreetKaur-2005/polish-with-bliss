# Color System

## 1. Introduction
### 1.1 Purpose
The purpose of the Color System is to establish a consistent, accessible, and emotionally resonant visual language for the Polish with Bliss digital experience. It ensures that every color choice reinforces the brand's commitment to luxury, tranquility, and professional care.

### 1.2 Color Philosophy
Our palette is rooted in the "Blissful Elegance" aesthetic—a harmonious blend of warm earth tones, soft florals, and metallic accents. It is designed to evoke the sensory experience of a high-end salon: calming, sophisticated, and warm.

---

## 2. Primary Palette
### 2.1 Primary Rose (Terracotta)
- **Hex**: `#d4a373`
- **Usage**: Primary brand color. Used for high-priority Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons, active navigation states, and primary iconography. It represents warmth and expertise.

### 2.2 Soft Blush
- **Hex**: `#fdf8f5`
- **Usage**: Large-scale surface backgrounds and secondary sections. It provides a soft, feminine, and welcoming atmosphere that differentiates us from clinical medical spas.

### 2.3 Rose Gold Accent
- **Hex**: `#e6c1b3` (approximation of the metallic logo tones)
- **Usage**: Used for delicate borders, decorative elements, and icon highlights to add a layer of premium luxury and boutique charm.

---

## 3. Neutral Palette
### 3.1 Background
- **Hex**: `#fff8f5` (Blush Cream)
- **Usage**: The primary site background, ensuring a bright and airy feel that promotes a sense of "tranquil clarity."

### 3.2 Surface
- **Hex**: `#ffffff` (Pure White)
- **Usage**: Used for cards, form fields, and elevated elements to create clean separation from the soft blush background.

### 3.3 Border
- **Hex**: `#dfd9d6`
- **Usage**: Subtle borders for cards and sections, providing structure without introducing visual clutter.

### 3.4 Divider
- **Hex**: `#f9f2ef`
- **Usage**: Horizontal rules and subtle separation between service items or list entries.

---

## 4. Typography Colors
### 4.1 Primary Text (Deep Espresso)
- **Hex**: `#2d2926`
- **Usage**: Used for all main headings (Playfair Display) and body text (Inter) to ensure maximum readability and a formal, high-contrast feel.

### 4.2 Secondary Text
- **Hex**: `#5b5652`
- **Usage**: Used for taglines, subheaders, and metadata. It reduces visual weight while maintaining accessibility.

### 4.3 Muted Text
- **Hex**: `#8c8580`
- **Usage**: Used for placeholder text in forms and legal copyright notices in the footer.

---

## 5. Functional Colors
### 5.1 Success
- **Hex**: `#7d9e7d` (Soft Sage)
- **Usage**: Confirmation messages for appointment requests and successful form submissions.

### 5.2 Error
- **Hex**: `#c97b7b` (Dusty Rose-Red)
- **Usage**: Validation errors in the booking form. Chosen to be noticeable without being alarmist, staying within the warm brand family.

### 5.3 Warning
- **Hex**: `#e0b88c` (Amber Gold)
- **Usage**: Important notifications or temporary service changes.

### 5.4 Information
- **Hex**: `#8ca6b8` (Slate Blue)
- **Usage**: Tooltips or general informational banners.

---

## 6. Component Usage
### 6.1 Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Terracotta (`#d4a373`) with white text.
- **Secondary**: Outlined Rose Gold with Terracotta text.
- **Hover**: 10% opacity reduction or slight scale.

### 6.2 Forms
- **Input BG**: Pure White (`#ffffff`).
- **Focus Border**: Terracotta (`#d4a373`) with a soft glow.
- **Labels**: Deep Espresso (`#2d2926`).

### 6.3 Cards
- **Background**: Pure White or Soft Cream.
- **Shadow**: Subtle Rose Gold tinted shadow to add depth without grayness.

### 6.4 Navigation
- **Text**: Deep Espresso.
- **Active State**: Terracotta underline or text weight shift.
- **Sticky State**: Backdrop blur with 80% Blush Cream opacity.

### 6.5 Footer
- **Background**: Deep Espresso (`#2d2926`).
- **Text**: Blush Pink (`#fdf8f5`) for high contrast and readability on dark backgrounds.

---

## 7. Accessibility
### 7.1 Contrast Ratios
- **Body Text**: Maintain 4.5:1 against all surface colors.
- **Headings**: Maintain 3:1 against background colors.
- **CTAs**: Ensure button text meets AA standards against Terracotta.

### 7.2 Color Usage Guidelines
- Never use color alone to convey meaning (e.g., error states must include text or an icon).
- Ensure Terracotta is not used for small text on light backgrounds to maintain contrast.

### 7.3 Do's and Don'ts
- **Do**: Use generous white space to allow the palette to breathe.
- **Do**: Use Rose Gold primarily for decoration and subtle borders.
- **Don't**: Use harsh black (`#000000`) for text; always use Deep Espresso.
- **Don't**: Overuse the Terracotta accent; it should be reserved for points of interaction.
